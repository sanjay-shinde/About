5 Hot Announcements from Dreamforce 2026

By Sanjay Shinde · Sep 2026 · 6 min read Agentforce AI Data 360

Every Dreamforce sets the tone for the year ahead — and Dreamforce 2026 made one thing unmistakably clear: the platform is now built agent-first. From a purpose-built reasoning model to a landmark AI partnership, here are the five announcements I think every architect, admin, and developer should have on their radar.

1

Agentforce 360

The platform, reframed around autonomous agents.

Agentforce 360 is the headline evolution of Salesforce's agent platform — a unified layer for building, deploying, and governing AI agents across every customer-facing surface. Salesforce showcased it at real enterprise scale (including a marquee collaboration with FIDE to power the future of chess), signalling that agents are moving from pilots to production across sales, service, marketing, and commerce.

Why it matters for architects: agents become first-class citizens in your solution design. Topics, actions, guardrails, and observability now sit alongside your objects, flows, and Apex as core building blocks.

2

Koa — the first CRM reasoning model

A model built for enterprise decision-making, not just chat.

Koa is Salesforce's inaugural CRM-specific reasoning model, developed with NVIDIA on Nemotron technology. Rather than a general-purpose LLM, Koa is tuned for the kinds of structured, multi-step decisions CRM work actually demands — grounded in your data and business context.

A reasoning model that understands CRM semantics out of the box is a big deal: it shortens the gap between "the model can talk" and "the agent can reliably act."
3

Claudeforce — Salesforce × Anthropic

Claude, brought natively into the platform.

One of the most talked-about announcements: Claudeforce, a strategic partnership that integrates Anthropic's Claude models into the Salesforce platform. It's a strong statement on model choice — giving customers access to frontier reasoning while keeping everything inside Salesforce's trust and governance boundaries.

The theme to watch: multi-model. Salesforce is leaning into letting customers pick the right model for the job rather than betting on a single one.

4

Missionforce

Agentforce for government and national security.

Missionforce brings purpose-built AI agents to the public sector and defense space, with the security posture those missions require (including IL5-level authorization). It's Salesforce meeting some of the most demanding compliance and sovereignty requirements head-on — a signal of how seriously the company is taking regulated, high-stakes deployments.

5

Data 360

The data foundation that makes agents trustworthy.

None of the above works without great data — and Data 360 is the unified data and AI infrastructure that grounds agents in a complete, real-time view of the customer. For anyone who has worked with Data Cloud, this is the layer that turns scattered records into the harmonized, resolved context an agent needs to answer correctly and act safely.

Architect's lens: your data model, identity resolution, and harmonization work is no longer "nice to have" — it's the difference between an agent that helps and one that hallucinates.

My take: three things to do next

  • Get your data house in order. Data 360 rewards clean models, solid identity resolution, and clear governance.
  • Prototype an agent now. Pick one high-friction workflow and stand up an Agentforce 360 agent end to end.
  • Think multi-model. With Koa and Claudeforce, design for choosing the right model per use case — and for governing them all.

Dreamforce 2026 wasn't about a single flashy feature — it was about a platform decisively reorganizing itself around agents, grounded data, and model choice. The organizations that invest in the fundamentals now will be the ones extracting real value a year from today.
